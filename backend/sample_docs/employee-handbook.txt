EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK – GENERAL INFORMATION

Working hours
The standard working week is Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local office time. Teams may agree flexible start and end times with their manager. Employees should record attendance using the approved company attendance system.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave should be requested through the HR portal and approved by the employee's manager before travel is booked. Unused leave is handled according to the employment agreement and applicable local law. For sick leave or emergencies, notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible and submit the required HR portal request when able.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely when their role and team arrangements permit it. Employees working remotely must remain reachable during agreed collaboration hours, protect company information, and use approved devices and accounts. Remote-work arrangements should be agreed with the manager.

Travel expenses
Business travel must be approved by the manager before booking. Employees should submit itemised receipts and an expense claim through the finance portal within 15 calendar days after completing the trip. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

IT support
For account, laptop, network, or software issues, create a ticket in the internal IT service desk. Include the device asset tag, a short description, and screenshots when appropriate. Never share passwords or one-time verification codes with support staff.
