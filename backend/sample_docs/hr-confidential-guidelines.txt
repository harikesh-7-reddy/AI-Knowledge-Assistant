HUMAN RESOURCES – CONFIDENTIAL GUIDELINES

Compensation review cycle
The company conducts a compensation review once each year. Managers submit recommendations to HR, and final changes require approval from the authorised compensation committee. Individual salary information is confidential and must only be shared with authorised HR personnel and the employee's designated management chain where permitted.

Recruitment records
Candidate resumes, interview notes, assessment results, and contact information are restricted HR records. Access is limited to the assigned recruitment team and approved hiring managers. Recruitment records must not be copied into public channels or shared outside the approved hiring process.

Employee relations
Employee relations cases and investigation notes are confidential. Access is limited to designated HR staff and other explicitly authorised reviewers. Employees should contact the HR service desk to raise a private concern.
